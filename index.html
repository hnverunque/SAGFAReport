Organization Overview

Who is SAGFA?

The Sitio Ansay General Farmers Association is a community-based farmers' association uniting smallholder farmers of Sitio Ansay and Brgy. Union in Caibiran, Biliran.

Brief History

Officially established on January 10, 2022, SAGFA was formed to ensure farmers' needs were recognized and supported by the government. Individual farmers had previously struggled to access training programs, crop insurance, and financial assistance.

The association started with no capital — members contributed a ₱50 membership fee and ₱20 monthly dues. Through cooperation, SAGFA built a "payag" (common facility) and began income-generating activities.

Jan 2022

Association officially established with no initial capital. Members begin contributing membership fees and dues.

2022–2023

Received government support: 480-layer chickens, seeds, organic fertilizers, grass cutter, shredder, thresher, and a triwheel service vehicle.

May 2024

Expanded into egg production through a grant from the AMIA (Adaptation and Mitigation Initiative in Agriculture) program. Vision & Mission updated to include egg production.

2026

17 active members continue to grow productivity and livelihoods through vegetable farming, root crops, poultry, and organic fertilizer production.

🌿

Core Products

Vegetables, root crops, eggs, and organic fertilizers — all produced sustainably for the local community.

🤝

Cooperative Culture

All members participate in decision-making through monthly meetings. Transparency in finances and shared responsibility define SAGFA's operations.

🏛️

Government Partnerships

Key partnerships with the Department of Agriculture and the AMIA program provide equipment, training, seeds, and livelihood support.

Case Report 1

Vision & Mission Analysis

Evaluating SAGFA's guiding statements, identifying best practices in participatory governance, and proposing improved versions.

Current Statements

Current Vision

"To be a progressive and sustainable association committed to enhancing the production of vegetables, root crops, and eggs, contributing to food security, improved livelihoods, and community development."

Current Mission

  • Strengthen the production of safe, nutritious, and high-quality vegetables, root crops, and eggs.
  • Equip members with knowledge, skills, and resources to improve productivity and efficiency.
  • Promote cooperation, innovation, and sustainability among members.
  • Support livelihood opportunities and economic growth within the community.
  • Contribute to a reliable and affordable food supply.

Analysis

The vision is future-oriented and sustainability-focused, but slightly lengthy. The mission clearly identifies products and emphasizes cooperation, yet lacks a specific target market and measurable goals. Overall, strong alignment exists between statements and actual operations.

✦ Improved Vision

"To be the local community's trusted source of safe, affordable, and sustainable agricultural products that strengthen food security and improve livelihoods among its members."

✦ Improved Mission (Key Additions)

Explicitly names customers (local households, sari-sari stores, grocery departments), integrates technology (automated inventory, modern equipment), and adds financial sustainability as a goal — addressing all 9 David & David mission components.

Best Practice Highlighted

SAGFA uses participatory governance — all members attend monthly meetings, a secretary documents concerns and suggestions, and drafts are refined collectively until finalized. This inclusive approach strengthens commitment and ensures alignment with shared goals.

Case Report 6

Strategy Implementation

Examining current implementation practices across functional areas and providing targeted recommendations.

📣

Marketing Issues

Relies on word-of-mouth; no formal branding, packaging, or labeling. Products lack differentiation and the association has no structured promotion channels.

Recommendation

Establish a Facebook page, introduce product labeling, and maintain consistent pricing and quality to enhance visibility and credibility cost-efficiently.

💰

Finance Issues

Transparent but manual. Basic bookkeeping and cash tracking in meetings. No formal budgeting, forecasting, or performance metrics.

Recommendation

Adopt spreadsheet-based accounting tools to track income, expenses, and cash flow. Introduce basic budgeting and periodic financial reviews.

🔬

R&D Issues

Experience-based and adaptive but not formally documented. Insights from trial-and-error may not be systematically shared or replicated.

Recommendation

Begin documenting farming practices, experiments, and outcomes — production techniques, input usage, and yield results — to build a knowledge base.

💻

MIS Issues

Manual records and verbal communication; insufficient for growth. No formal MIS results in delayed reporting and limited data accuracy.

Recommendation

Implement a basic Excel-based MIS covering: inventory tracking, egg production monitoring, and sales/revenue recording.

Case Report 8

Strategy Evaluation

Documenting SAGFA's evaluation practices, auditing activities, contingency planning, and recommending a Balanced Scorecard framework.

Current Evaluation Practices

Evaluation is informal, participatory, and discussion-based. Monitoring occurs during regular meetings where members review activities and assess outcomes collectively. Criteria are largely qualitative: production improvement, member participation, effective resource use, and operational continuity.

Issue

No formal performance indicators or quantitative benchmarks. Depends heavily on perception and experience rather than systematic measurement.

Contingency Planning

SAGFA's approach is largely reactive. Challenges are addressed through collective problem-solving and external support. No formal contingency plans increase vulnerability to climate change, market fluctuations, and resource dependency.

Recommendation

Develop basic contingency plans for: climate-related risks (crop diversification), financial risks (emergency funds), and operational disruptions (backup production plans).
